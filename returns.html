Returns & Exchanges

Last updated 27 October 2026

Every piece at Ketra’s Kloset is one of one, so we keep returns simple and fair. Please read this before you order.

1. Return window

You can ask to return a piece within 7 days of receiving it. Message us within those 7 days, even if the piece reaches us a little later.

2. What can be returned

  • Pieces must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered and free of make-up, perfume or deodorant marks.
  • All original tags must still be attached.
  • Sale pieces are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged, unless they arrive damaged or are not what you ordered.
  • For hygiene reasons, bodysuits, swimwear and pierced jewellery (earrings) are final sale unless faulty.
  • Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

3. How to start a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp +256 700 929 189 or email contact@ketraskloset.com with your name, the piece and why you’re returning it. Photos help.
  2. We’ll confirm whether the piece is eligible and agree how it gets back to us (drop-off or a rider in Kampala).
  3. Once it arrives we check it, usually within 2 days, and tell you the outcome.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to the Airtel Money number you paid from (or another mobile-money number you confirm in writing), within 3 working days of us receiving and checking the piece. Original delivery fees are not refunded unless the piece was faulty or wrong.

5. Exchanges

Because every piece is one of one, we can’t swap for the same item in another size. You can exchange for a different available piece; we’ll settle any price difference by Airtel Money. If you prefer, we can issue store credit as a gift card instead.

6. Damaged or wrong items

If a piece arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We’ll arrange collection at our cost and offer a full refund (including delivery), an exchange, or store credit — your choice.

7. Return delivery costs

For change-of-mind returns, you pay to get the piece back to us. If the mistake is ours, we pay.

8. Loyalty points

Kloset Circle points earned on a returned piece are removed. Points you used on an order that is refunded are put back on your account.

9. Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or use our contact page. We’re happy to help.

See also our Privacy Policy.
